# Known before: AGG = Met in *Porphyridium aerugineum* mitochondria (literature check, 2026-11-11)

Searches (WebSearch extended + standard, 2026-11-11): "Porphyridium aerugineum mitochondrial genome AGG codon methionine
genetic code"; "red algae mitochondrial genetic code reassignment AGG arginine 2024 2025 2026". No paper reports a
non-standard AGG in any red alga. Hits were the green-algal papers below, arthropod AGG Ser/Lys, and the SAG culture page
(SAG 1380-2 mito genome MZ748310, 26,688 bp; no code analysis).

- NCBI code tables 1-33 (gc.prt v4.6, parsed earlier for the AGA lead): no table has AGG = Met.
- Noutahi et al. 2019 MBE ("Rapid genetic code evolution in green algal mitochondrial genomes") and Zihala & Elias 2019
  GBE 11:2992: AGG = Met in *Chromochloris zofingiensis* (Sphaeropleales, green alga).
- Shulgina & Eddy 2021 eLife 10:e71402: AGG = Met in uncultivated Bacilli and two phage assemblies from the same samples (bacteria only).
- Shulgina & Eddy 2023 (Codetta mitogenome survey): no red algal AGG call (as recorded in README).
- Genome papers: van Beveren et al. 2022 GBE (SAG 1380-2, NC_062302.1) and Kim et al. 2022 BMC Biol. 20:2 (first author Kim D; earlier written as "Lee" in error) (CCMP1948,
  MT483995.1): neither mentions a code change (as recorded in README; checked earlier in this project).
- Barcyte, Zihala & Elias (PLoS Genet. 2025): AGA/AGG = Ala in pedinophytes (green), not Met.
